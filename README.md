
# Overview

FoodHub is a food aggregator platform that connects customers with restaurants for seamless food ordering and delivery. The company aims to analyze order data to improve service efficiency and customer satisfaction.

# Objectives

🔹 Analyze order trends and customer preferences.

🔹 Identify key factors affecting delivery efficiency.

🔹 Provide recommendations for optimizing business operations.

# Dataset

Key Features: Order ID, Customer ID, Restaurant Name, Cuisine Type, Cost, Day of the Week, Rating, Food Preparation Time, Delivery Time.

Size: 1898 rows × 9 columns

# Data Analysis & Insights

✅ Most Popular Cuisine: American, followed by Japanese and Italian.

✅ Orders Peak on Weekends: 71% of orders are placed on weekends.

✅ Delivery Time is Longer on Weekdays: Average 28.34 min (Weekday) vs. 22.47 min (Weekend).

✅ Customer Ratings: 39% of orders have missing ratings.

✅ High-Cost Orders: 29% of orders cost over $20.

✅ Frequent Customers: Top 3 customers placed 13, 10, and 9 orders, respectively.

✅ Top Restaurants by Order Volume: Shake Shack, The Meatball Shop, Blue Ribbon Sushi.

✅ Revenue: Total revenue from commissions is $6166.30.

✅ Delivery Efficiency: 10.5% of orders take over 60 minutes.

# Key Recommendations

📌 Address Missing Ratings: Implement a simpler rating system (e.g., thumbs up/down) to improve feedback.

📌 Optimize Delivery on Weekdays: Identify causes for longer delivery times and allocate more drivers.

📌 Boost Weekday Orders: Introduce promotions or discounts to encourage orders.

📌 Expand Japanese Cuisine Options: Highest orders per restaurant indicate demand.

📌 Improve Order Distribution: Balance restaurant supply to meet demand more efficiently.

# Conclusion

FoodHub can leverage these insights to enhance customer experience, streamline operations, and optimize marketing efforts. Future improvements could include predictive modeling for demand forecasting and real-time delivery optimization.

Technologies Used

🛠 Python, Pandas, Seaborn, Matplotlib.
